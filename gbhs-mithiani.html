About Our School

A Beacon of
Education in Mithiani

Government Boys' Higher Secondary School Mithiani, Campus proudly serves the community with free, accessible, and quality education from primary through intermediate level. Affiliated with the Board of Secondary Education Sindh, our school has been a cornerstone institution for generations of students across the region.

With dedicated faculty, a structured curriculum, and a focus on both academic excellence and moral development, we prepare our students not just for examinations — but for life.
